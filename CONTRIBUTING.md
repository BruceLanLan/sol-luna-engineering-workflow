# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they make the workflow clearer, safer, easier to verify, or more portable across Codex versions.

Please keep pull requests focused and include:

- the workflow problem being solved;
- the exact files changed;
- compatibility assumptions;
- validation performed;
- whether the change affects routing, model effort, delegation safety, or final acceptance.

Do not weaken the core boundaries: Luna must not make unresolved architecture or security decisions, and Luna must not perform final acceptance of its own work.
